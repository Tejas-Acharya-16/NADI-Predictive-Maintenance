# Baseline reproduction

The previous selected HGB configuration, capped at 125 with causal 10/30 features, was rerun on a stronger five-fold engine-grouped validation protocol with fixed 10/25/50/75/100-cycle pseudo-censoring horizons. It achieved pooled RMSE 10.063, MAE 6.799, R2 0.905, and PHM08 mean 2.032. These metrics differ from the older fixed 60-case split because the splits and endpoint schedule are deliberately different.
